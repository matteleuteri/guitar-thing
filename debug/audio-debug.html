Recorded body IR

  
The synth's "box" is a static EQ — the one thing keeping it from sounding
    like a guitar (progress 13: synthetic body IRs wobbled by ear, twice). Capture a real one:
    click Capture body IR, wait for the prompt, knock gently on the top near the bridge.
    It auto-finds the knock, auto-crops, and applies it to the whole synth. (That's the one-click
    path. Fine control: record normally, crop Start/End over the strip, then "Set crop as body IR".)

Guitar engine

  
Which engine produces guitar voicings. smplr kit plays a real GM
    steel-guitar sample (self-hosted assets/guitar-steel-ogg.js, ~2.6 MB,
    decoded once into six per-string instruments); recorded samples plays your
    open-string recordings below; K–S synth is the Karplus–Strong fallback. The
    kit is pre-loaded on page load; a play that lands mid-decode waits for it (up to
    ~8s) so the first sound is the kit — only a stuck/failed kit falls back.

Sample playback (your own guitar)

  
Stop synthesizing a guitar and just use yours: record the six open
    strings once, each in one click. Every voicing is then played by transposing the
    nearest recorded string to the fretted note, so the whole fretboard comes out
    of these six recordings (~a few semitones max). Pluck cleanly, hold, let it ring,
    press the button again to stop. Clear the body IR above too — samples carry their
    own body.
