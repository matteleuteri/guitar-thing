Note/Chord Finder

Give it a set of notes: it highlights every spot on the fretboard where those notes live, then finds chord fingerings (muted strings allowed) where every chord note is played and all fretted positions lie within a small fret span.

Give it a chord progression: it picks one voicing per chord and chains them so the whole song shifts as little as possible, highlighting the notes you can keep held as it changes.
